Simple rules

  • Registration: ₹9 per application. Payment does not guarantee approval. The fee is non-refundable except where applicable law, payment-provider rules, or a confirmed duplicate/failed payment require otherwise.
  • Approval: Submit correct details and proof. Your User ID and coupon code are provided after approval.
  • Customer discount: Customers using an approved partner's code get 10% off eligible product value. Checkout determines the final discount. The code cannot be used for the partner's own purchases.
  • Partner reward: Partners receive reward coins only. There is no 10% reward cash, cash commission or separate cash reward.
  • Coins: Credited after eligible payment is confirmed. Refunds, returns, cancellations or chargebacks may reverse the related rewards/coins.
  • Withdrawals: Minimum available balance ₹1,000. Submit a request; payout needs verification and admin approval. Processing normally takes 5–7 business days after complete details and approval. Bank settlement may take longer.
  • Code validity: No fixed expiry while your account is approved and code remains active. Misuse or fraud may lead to suspension.
  • No guaranteed income: Earnings depend on eligible orders. Do not make false product claims or spam customers.

By applying, you confirm that you have read and understood the programme rules. Humor Luxury may review orders, correct errors, reverse ineligible rewards and suspend accounts for misuse, subject to applicable law.
